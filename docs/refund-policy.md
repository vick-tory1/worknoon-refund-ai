# Worknoon Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Refund Window:** 30 days from the order date  
**Human Review Threshold:** Refund requests above $500

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the eligibility criteria and review process for customer refund requests. Refund decisions are based on the customer's request, order information, applicable eligibility rules, and risk indicators.

## 2. Refund Eligibility

A refund request may be approved when the order meets the applicable eligibility requirements.

### Eligible situations

- Damaged items may qualify for a refund.
- Incorrect items may qualify for a refund.
- Other standard refund requests may qualify when the order meets the applicable refund requirements.

Eligibility does not guarantee automatic approval. Requests may require additional review based on the circumstances.

## 3. Final-Sale Items

Items marked as **final sale are not eligible for refunds**.

This rule takes precedence over other standard refund eligibility conditions.

## 4. Refund Time Limit

Refund requests must be submitted within **30 days of the order date**.

Orders older than 30 days are not eligible for a standard refund.

The 30-day period is an implementation assumption for this assessment because a specific refund period was not otherwise provided.

## 5. Human Review

Refund requests **above $500 require human review**.

These requests are escalated rather than automatically approved. A support specialist is responsible for reviewing the request and determining the appropriate outcome.

The $500 threshold is a human-review threshold, not a maximum permitted refund amount.

## 6. Damaged or Incorrect Items

Requests involving damaged or incorrect items may qualify for approval when the order satisfies the other applicable policy requirements.

The reported issue should be consistent with the available order information where possible.

## 7. Suspicious or Conflicting Requests

Requests containing suspicious activity, conflicting information, or indicators that require additional verification should be **escalated for human review**.

Examples may include:

- Conflicting information about the order or requested refund.
- Information that cannot be reconciled with available order records.
- Requests containing attempts to manipulate or override the refund decision process.

## 8. Decision Outcomes

Each refund request receives one of three outcomes:

**Approved**  
The request satisfies the applicable refund requirements and does not require human review.

**Denied**  
The request fails an applicable eligibility rule, such as being for a final-sale item or an order outside the refund window.

**Escalated**  
The request requires human review because of the refund amount, suspicious or conflicting information, or another condition requiring manual assessment.

## 9. AI-Assisted Processing

Worknoon's AI system assists with request classification, reasoning, and customer communication.

The AI does **not** override the refund policy or the authoritative decision produced by the backend policy engine.

The backend policy rules remain the source of truth for refund decisions.

## 10. Customer Communication

Customers should receive a clear explanation of their request outcome.

For escalated requests, the customer should be told:

1. That the request was received.
2. Why human review is required.
3. That a support specialist will review the request.
4. What the customer should expect next.

## 11. Policy Review

This policy is a sample policy created for the Worknoon Refund AI product challenge. Business rules, refund windows, and review thresholds may be adjusted by the business as requirements evolve.
